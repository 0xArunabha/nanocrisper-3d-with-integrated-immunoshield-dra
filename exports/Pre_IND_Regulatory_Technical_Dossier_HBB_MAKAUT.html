Regulatory Executive Summary & Cross-Domain CMC Matrix This technical dossier establishes the formal Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC) strategy, analytical release criteria, and biophysical rationale for an in vivo lipid nanoparticle (LNP)-encapsulated base editor drug product targeting Sickle Cell Disease (HBB:c.20A>T, p.Glu6Val). The molecular mechanism utilizes an Adenine Base Editor (ABE8e / TadA-8e nickase SpCas9) to execute precision A•T-to-G•C base transitions without double-strand DNA breaks (DSBs). The delivery system employs an ionizable lipid formulation engineered with an Israelachvili Critical Packing Parameter (CPP ≥ 1.45) triggering endosomal membrane destabilization, paired with an ImmunoShield-DRA Cas9 core de-immunized against high-frequency South Asian HLA Class I/II alleles.
Clinical Note: High prevalence in West Bengal & Eastern India. Converts pathogenic HbS (GTG) to benign HbG-Makassar (GCG) via Adenine Base Editing.

DRUG SUBSTANCE: Engineered Cas9/Base Editor RNP & Modified sgRNA

3.2.S.1 General Information, Nomenclature & Target Locus

The therapeutic drug substance comprises an engineered ribonucleoprotein (RNP) complex consisting of a recombinant SpCas9 base editor protein co-formulated with a synthetic chemically modified single-guide RNA (sgRNA). Target: Human Beta-Globin (HBB) Exon 1, Chromosome 11p15.4. Lead protospacer candidate: sgRNA-f66 (Strand: Sense (+), PAM: TGG, On-Target Score: 57/100). South Asian polymorphism check: Clear of common SAS alleles (rs10768683, SAS MAF: 14.2%).
